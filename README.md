# HERD International : Research Data Portal

Explore HERD International's research data resources from **2014 to 2026**.

## What you can find here

Browse our catalogue of research datasets, collection tools, and resources across **13 years** of projects.

### Data available by year

2014 · 2015 · 2016 · 2017 · 2018 · 2019 · 2020 · 2021 · 2022 · 2023 · 2024 · 2025 · 2026

### Categorised by

- **Project** — national and international research projects
- **Data collection tools** — questionnaires, survey tools, field manuals
- **Datasets** — household surveys, census data, health equity and population data
- **Reports & publications** — project reports, policy briefs, factsheets
- **Digital tools & code** — analysis scripts (R / Python), GIS resources

## How to use

1. **Search** the catalogue by project name
2. **Filter** by year
3. See at a glance:
   - 📁 which project and topic
   - 📄 which file types are available
   - 🔓 whether a file is publicly downloadable or available on request

## Access

| Status | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Public download** | The file can be downloaded immediately |
| **Request access** | Click "Request access" and fill the short form |

When you submit a request, HERD International's data team reviews your purpose and replies within a few working days.
