# HERD International — Research Data Portal

**Live Portal:** [https://herd-data-portal.github.io/herd-data-portal/](https://herd-data-portal.github.io/herd-data-portal/)

Welcome to **HERD International's Research Data Portal & Centre for Data Science Hub**. This platform helps researchers, students, and partners discover approved research resources, build practical data science skills, and collaborate on public health and development evidence in Nepal and beyond.

---

## What You Can Explore

* **Data Catalogue**  
  Browse HERD International research projects by year, thematic area, and resource type. View project summaries, download approved public reports, tools, and outputs, or request access to restricted datasets.

* **Data Science & AI Hub**  
  A capacity-building space for students, graduates, and researchers featuring core learning pillars in Data Science, Machine Learning, GIS, and Responsible AI—alongside side-by-side **Stata, R, and Python** code examples and recommended open-source tools.

* **Standards & Impact**  
  A curated directory of Nepal and global public health data sources, data quality and de-identification standards, citation guidelines, synthetic practice datasets for hands-on learning, and pathways for joint studies and student research.

---

## Data Access & Ethics

Publication and data access are separate decisions. Only explicitly approved resources receive a public download link. Restricted datasets remain protected and may be requested through the portal's **Request** link, subject to project-owner review and ethical data-sharing terms.
